event-hub prototypes

A public page and three click-through prototypes that start at login. Nothing is saved, and all names and data are made up.

Public events page

What anyone sees without logging in: the upcoming events, and a form for proposing a talk. A proposal lands in the organizers' potential speakers, marked as applied themselves.

Speaker prototype

From the invitation email and setting a password through to the day of the event and after.

Organizer prototype

From potential speakers, who applied themselves or were added by an organizer, and inviting one, through review and publishing, to the thank-you.

Administrator prototype

Setting the community's name and logo, which replace the product name on every screen, managing the organizers and administrators who can log in, and their own profile.

One person, several roles

Ada Example is a speaker, an organizer and an administrator, with one login and one profile. Log in as Ada, then open the account menu at the top right to switch role. Each role opens on the screen she used last.
