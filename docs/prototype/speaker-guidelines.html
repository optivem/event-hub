Speaker Guidelines

Tech Excellence. These help us run each event well and give everyone a good experience. If you have questions, ask your coordinator.

Presentation materials

Please send us all the materials you have:

  • Slides, such as Google Slides or PowerPoint
  • GitHub repository
  • Miro
  • Other: please tell the organizers what you'll use

If you're showing code without live coding, send the repository you'll show.

If you're live coding, send a repository with the start state and the end state, for example one branch for each.

If materials don't arrive by the due date in our emails, the organizers may cancel the event.

Ground rules

These cover your talk and Q&A, including speech, text, images, video and code. Our sessions are mainly technical, and we stay on the conservative side on other topics. We use this list to review your materials. If a rule is broken, we may cancel the event or delete the video from YouTube.

Values and beliefs
Avoid politics, vaccinations, laws, global conflicts and controversial events. Avoid negative comments on religious views, and analogies between religion and programming. Avoid references to other beliefs, including magic, astrology, superstition, idols, meditation and yoga.
Personal attacks
Disagree with ideas, not people. Avoid insulting or putting down people who have a different view.
Inappropriate language
Avoid off-color jokes, swearing and vulgarity.
NDA violations
Avoid revealing details of your company's projects or products, unless your company has explicitly given you permission.
Copyright violations
Don't reuse presentations or code you made for an employer or client. Don't plagiarize, and quote your sources. Only use images you're allowed to use, and credit the source.
Discrimination
Avoid discrimination against people, such as racial or gender discrimination.
Sales pitches
The session is there to inform and educate. You can briefly mention your products or services in your intro and at the end.
Presentation materials
Make sure your materials reflect your title and agenda.

Advance preparation

Please do these a few weeks or months before the event:

Meetup group
Join our Meetup group, so that we can add you as a co-host. You can also follow us on social media.
Profile photo
Check that you have a professional photo on LinkedIn, preferably a headshot with no distracting background. Tell us if you'd like us to use another photo. We may use another of your online photos if your LinkedIn one doesn't fit, and we may make small corrections such as cropping.
Timelines
Accept the calendar invite, and send your agenda and materials by the due dates in our emails. We may edit and comment on your agenda. If you're late, we may cancel your session.

Timing

Your calendar invite covers the full length, including preparation and live streaming. Please arrive on time.
