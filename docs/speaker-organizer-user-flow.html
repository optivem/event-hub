Gaps in the procedure

Each gap shows the recommended handling, which is a proposal until organizers agree it. It is drawn in the diagram unless marked otherwise. To agree or change it, raise it in #organizers.

RefTopicGap and current treatment
G1Speaker-initiated proposalsThe procedure doesn't cover them. Drawn: a second way into Filtering, decided by the other organizers.
G2Returning speakersThe design covers a new speaker's account. Recommended: a returning speaker gets the same invitation and signs in.
G3No reply to the invitation“Ignore them” ends the path but says nothing about the slot. Recommended: the slot goes back to Filtering for the next speaker.
G4Reminder after 1 weekThe in-app design lists automatic reminders at 1 month and 3 weeks only. Recommended: make this one an automatic email too.
G5Speaker can't make the dateThe template has a single “last slot” email and marks “tell us which months” as ignore, but in practice dates went back and forth. Recommended: the loop, until no slot is left.
G6Checks with no follow-upAccepting the calendar invite and joining the Meetup group are requested but never checked. Recommended: keep them as speaker tasks, with no check.
G7Disagreeing with the guidelinesSpeakers proceed “if you agree”, but nothing says what happens otherwise. Recommended, not drawn: the speaker raises it with the coordinator, and the path ends if they can't agree.
G8What the checks look for“Timing” checks materials only, while reminders chase agenda and materials. One template calls materials required, another optional. Recommended: both are required and checked.
G9Due date after a rescheduleWhen an event moved 1 week earlier, the due date stayed, so it fell about 3 weeks before the event. Recommended: a reschedule goes back through date confirmation, which resets the due date to 1 month before the new date.
G10CancellationThe final reminder warns of cancellation, but “Timing” has no cancellation step. Recommended: no agenda or materials by 2 weeks before cancels the event.
G11Conflicting final deadlineA leftover draft sets a last deadline that publishes 1 week before the event, not 2. Recommended: 2 weeks.
G12Review doesn't passNo source says what happens. Recommended: the speaker updates within 1 week and it is reviewed again. Not drawn: a missed update cancels the event, as in G10.
G13Publish order“Timing” publishes before the THIRD EMAIL, and the template says Meetup is already live. In practice it went out first and everything was announced the next day. Recommended: follow the practice.
G14Publishing ownerA leftover note gives LinkedIn and Twitter to one organizer. Recommended: keep it under Other organizers, with no separate lane.
G15Speaker drops out after the announcementThe template says the event is cancelled and another speaker arranged, but no step covers it. Recommended: an end point, with the coordinator finding the replacement.
G16Day-of and post-event stepsThese come only from leftover drafts and one sample, so they are drawn with dotted borders as unconfirmed. When the co-host is added is also unclear. Recommended: confirm them with organizers, and add the co-host on the event day before 17:00.
G17Waiting after the reminderNothing says how long to wait for a reply after the reminder before the path ends. Recommended: 1 more week.
G18When review startsA speaker can hand everything in well before the 2-week check. Recommended: review starts once agenda and materials are in, and 2 weeks before the event at the latest.
