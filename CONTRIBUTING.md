## Contributor License Agreement

Before we can merge your first pull request, you need to sign the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). It lets Optivem distribute your contribution under the AGPL and under other licenses, including commercial ones. You keep the copyright in your work.

Signing is automatic. When you open a pull request, a bot comments with instructions. Reply with:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

You only need to sign once.
